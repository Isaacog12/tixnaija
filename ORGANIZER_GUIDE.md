# TixNaija Organizer Guide

Welcome to the TixNaija Organizer Guide! This document explains how to use the powerful back-office features and customization tools available to you as an event organizer. 

---

## 1. Event Customization

Stand out from the crowd by fully customizing your event pages and checkout experience. 

### Custom Event Themes & URLs
* **What it is:** Personalize your event page to match your brand.
* **How to use it:** When creating or editing an event, scroll to the **"Event Customization"** section.
  * **Event URL (Slug):** Choose a custom link for your event (e.g., `tixnaija.com/e/my-awesome-party`). 
  * **Theme Color:** Pick a primary brand color. Buttons, accents, and links on your event page will automatically adopt this color to give your buyers a cohesive brand experience.

### Custom Checkout Questions
* **What it is:** Collect specific information from your attendees during the checkout process (e.g., dietary restrictions, t-shirt sizes, or how they heard about the event).
* **How to use it:** In the "Event Customization" section of the event editor, click **"Add Question"**.
  * You can choose between **Short Text** or **Multiple Choice** (Dropdown) questions.
  * Check the **"Required"** box if attendees must answer the question before buying a ticket.
  * *Note:* You can view attendees' answers later in the **Manage Attendees** dashboard!

### Custom Order Confirmations
* **What it is:** Add a personalized message to the confirmation screen and ticket email that buyers receive after a successful purchase.
* **How to use it:** In the "Event Customization" section, fill out the **"Custom Order Confirmation Message"** box. You can include links to WhatsApp groups, venue instructions, or special thank-you notes.

### Event Tags
* **What it is:** Categorize your event with hashtags so buyers can easily discover it.
* **How to use it:** When creating/editing an event, type relevant keywords into the **"Tags"** field and press Enter (e.g., `Afrobeats`, `Tech`, `VIP`). Tags will be displayed on the Browse page and your public event page.

## 2. Back Office & Operations

TixNaija provides a full suite of operational tools to help you manage your events post-creation.

### Payouts & Earnings Dashboard
* **What it is:** A dedicated dashboard to track your total revenue and expected payouts.
* **How to use it:** Go to your **Dashboard** and click the **"Payouts"** tab.
  * You'll see your **Gross Ticket Revenue** across all events.
  * You'll see your **Estimated Net Payout** (Gross revenue minus the ~10% platform fee).
  * **Important:** You must connect a Paystack Subaccount in your Settings to receive automated payouts. If you haven't done this, a warning will appear at the top of the Payouts tab.

### Advanced Event Analytics
* **What it is:** Get deep insights into how your event is performing.
* **How to use it:** In the Dashboard under the "Events" tab, click the **Analytics** icon (bar chart) next to any event. You can track:
  * **Page Views:** See how many people are visiting your event page.
  * **Conversion Rate:** The percentage of visitors who actually buy a ticket.
  * **Revenue Over Time:** A line chart showing your ticket sales trends.
  * **Sales by Ticket Tier:** A donut chart showing which ticket types (e.g., VIP vs Regular) are the most popular.

### Mass Emails (Broadcasts)
* **What it is:** Send announcements, venue changes, or thank-you emails to everyone who bought a ticket.
* **How to use it:** 
  1. In the Dashboard, click the **Manage Attendees** icon (two people) next to your event.
  2. Click the **"Message Attendees"** button (mail icon) near the search bar.
  3. Enter your Subject Line and Message.
  4. Click **Send to All**. TixNaija will automatically deduplicate emails (so people who bought 5 tickets only get 1 email) and securely deliver your message to their inbox.

### Managing Attendees & Reassigning Tickets
* **What it is:** View your guest list, search for specific tickets, and fix typos in attendee emails.
* **How to use it:** In the **Manage Attendees** dashboard, you can search by Name, Email, or Ticket Code. 
  * If an attendee entered the wrong email address, you can click the **Edit** icon next to their ticket to update their email.
  * *Bonus:* Updating their email will automatically resend their ticket confirmation to the new address!
  * You can also cancel tickets here if necessary.
