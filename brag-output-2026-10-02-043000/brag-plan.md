# TixNaija Mobile App - Brag Plan

## The 9-Question Rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   TixNaija Mobile App: An event ticketing and management platform for event organizers and attendees.
2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "The ultimate event experience."
3. **What is the visual hook?**
   The locked ember orange accent (`oklch(0.66 0.22 40)`) paired with a sleek dark mode (`#22201e`), framing a QR ticket scanner.
4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   The ticket scanning flow (opening an event, launching the scanner, and getting a "Ticket Valid" confirmation).
5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   ~15 seconds.
6. **What tone fits best?**
   Preset: `app-store`. Creative direction: Smooth, feature-card clean promo for a modern ticketing app.
7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Smooth corporate/electronic bed with snappy UI sound effects (swipes, camera shutter, and a satisfying success chime for the ticket scan).
8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "Scan tickets and manage your events on the go. The TixNaija mobile app is here. 🎟️"
9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   View event dashboard → tap "Scan Tickets" → camera viewfinder over a QR code → green "Valid Ticket" overlay.

## Storyboard

**Total length:** ~16 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (9:16) for social/App Store

### Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:03)
- **Visual:** A slick, dark glassmorphism card slides in displaying the TixNaija logo with a pulsing ember orange glow (`oklch(0.66 0.22 40)`).
- **Text:** "Your events. In your pocket."
- **Audio:** A deep bass drop or woosh.
- **Transition:** Quick upward swipe to Scene 2.

### Scene 2: The Core Feature (0:03 - 0:08)
- **Visual:** We see a stylized mockup of the mobile app's dashboard. A finger cursor taps "Scan Tickets".
- **Text:** "Manage seamlessly."
- **Audio:** UI click sound effect.

### Scene 3: The Action (0:08 - 0:12)
- **Visual:** The screen switches to a camera viewfinder overlay. It frames a QR code. A scanning laser sweeps down, and a bright green "✅ Ticket Valid" badge pops up.
- **Text:** "Scan in seconds."
- **Audio:** Camera scanning beep, followed by a satisfying success chime.

### Scene 4: Outro (0:12 - 0:16)
- **Visual:** The phone frame spins and scales down into the center of the screen. App Store and Google Play badges fade in below the TixNaija logo.
- **Text:** "Download TixNaija today."
- **Audio:** Final musical resolve.
