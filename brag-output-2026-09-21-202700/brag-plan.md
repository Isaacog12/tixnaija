# Brag Plan: TixNaija

## 1. What is the app?
TixNaija is Naija's premier ticket hub, providing a seamless platform to discover events, buy tickets, and host events with secure QR-code access and easy local payments.

## 2. What is the funniest or most impressive claim?
"Selling out has never been this ridiculously easy." and "QR tickets, zero drama."

## 3. What is the visual hook?
The glowing "ember orange" sunset gradient (`oklch(0.66 0.22 40)`) and the Apple-grade typography (`Cinzel` for headings) set against a dark glassmorphic background with animated glowing orbs.

## 4. What should be shown from the actual UI?
- The hero search bar ("Find Events").
- A ticket card with the date badge, glowing hover effect, and location.
- The "QR tickets, zero drama" bento box feature.

## 5. What is the shortest satisfying video?
18 seconds. Enough time to show the problem (boring ticketing), the solution (search & buy), and the result (QR code generated).

## 6. What tone fits best?
- **Preset**: `cinematic`
- **Direction**: A premium, dramatic trailer showcasing the sleek interface and high-end event experience.

## 7. What should the audio feel like?
Cinematic bed with a low swell, restrained motion-matched accents for UI clicks (like the "Find Events" button press), and subtle audio-reactive glow on the background orbs.

## 8. What should the share caption say?
"Discover the most seamless way to host and discover events in Nigeria with TixNaija. Buy tickets. Turn up."

## 9. What's the user flow worth showing?
Search for an event -> See the ticket card -> Tap to get QR code for entry.

---

## Storyboard

**Total Duration: ~18 seconds**

### Scene 1: The Hook (0s - 3s)
- **Visual**: Dark screen. A glowing ember orb (`bg-primary`) slowly pulses into view. The text fades in using `Cinzel` font: "Buy tickets."
- **Audio**: Deep bass swell, low cinematic drone starts.
- **Action**: Text "Turn up." slides in below with the sunset gradient mask. 

### Scene 2: The UI Reveal (3s - 8s)
- **Visual**: The glowing orb expands to reveal the Hero section search bar. A cursor types "Lagos Concert" and clicks "Find Events".
- **Audio**: Subtle typing SFX, followed by a crisp, satisfying "click" SFX.
- **Action**: The search bar smoothly transitions into displaying a sleek, glassmorphic Event Card (using the site's `border-border/30 bg-card/80 backdrop-blur-xl` style).

### Scene 3: The Flex (8s - 14s)
- **Visual**: Fast cuts highlighting the bento box features. 
  - Cut 1: "Pay with ease" (shows the credit card icon).
  - Cut 2: "QR tickets, zero drama" (shows a glowing QR code scaling in).
- **Audio**: Two synchronized heavy hits/whooshes on the beat.
- **Action**: The QR code glows with the primary accent color.

### Scene 4: The Outro (14s - 18s)
- **Visual**: The screen fades to the dark background. The `TixNaija` logo/text appears. "Selling out has never been this easy."
- **Audio**: The music swells to a final peak and fades out.
- **Action**: Hold on the logo for 2 seconds.
