# Brag Plan: TixNaija

## What is this app?
TixNaija is Nigeria's premier ticket hub, providing a seamless way to discover events and secure scannable QR tickets in seconds.

## The angle
The absolute frictionlessness of getting into the best events in Naija. The video frames TixNaija as an elegant, premium, zero-drama gateway to turning up.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A giant, elegant serif text "Buy tickets." fades in gracefully. Then, the text "Turn up." slides up with a sunset gradient glow, accompanied by a deep, satisfying bass drop.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The search bar UI gracefully sliding into view with a glowing "Find Events" button.
- Three elegant value proposition cards appearing sequentially: "QR tickets, zero drama", "Pay with ease", and "Sell in minutes".
- An event card with a floating date badge and a rich cover image tilting into view.

## Outro / punchline
"Selling out has never been this ridiculously easy." The TixNaija brand logo/text settles into the center with a subtle sunset ember streak.

## User flow worth showing
Search for an event -> See the upcoming event card -> Highlight the QR code ticket feature (zero drama). 

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: sleek, premium Nigerian event culture
- Interpretation: Smooth, deliberate pacing. Elegant typography with deep dark-mode contrast. Motion is confident but restrained, emphasizing the "zero drama" aspect.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: ~20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: oklch(0.14 0.02 40)
- Accent: oklch(0.85 0.15 85)
- Text: oklch(0.97 0.02 80)
- Primary: oklch(0.66 0.22 40)
- Display font: Cinzel
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: The translucent glass bento cards with the glowing ember orange gradients.

## Share copy (draft)
We've eliminated the friction from ticketing. Experience the most seamless way to discover and host events in Nigeria. Turn up with TixNaija.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a modern, sophisticated beat
- Music: polished, driving but understated electronic beat
- Music treatment: Smooth fade in, drops on the hook "Turn up", maintains a steady rhythm for the cards, fading out on the logo.
- Music cue guidance: 
  - 0:02 - Hook "Turn up" drops.
  - 0:06 - 0:10 - Beat grid window for the value prop cards revealing.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the sunset gradient text and card borders pulse very softly to the bass.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched; professional restraint with soft, premium glass-like ticks for UI elements.
- Audio-coupled moments: The sequential reveal of the three bento cards synced to the beat.
- Restraint rule: No chaotic swooshes or loud impacts. Keep it sounding like a luxury brand.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 4s
Large `Cinzel` display text centered on the dark background: "Buy tickets." (Hold 1s). 
Then, a sunset-gradient "Turn up." fades and slides up smoothly just below it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: Anticipation building into a satisfying, confident drop.
Audio-coupled idea: The gradient text glows slightly brighter on the beat drop.
Music: Driving electronic beat kicks in on "Turn up".
Transition mood: smooth crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Search — 4s
The premium glass-morphic search bar appears in the center, with "Search for events, artists, venues..." placeholder. A cursor glides in and clicks the glowing "Find Events" button.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor glides to the button and simulates a click with a subtle scale-down press effect.
Audio intent: Clean, tactile UI sound for the click.
Audio-coupled idea: The button emits a soft glow when clicked.
Transition mood: slide up → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Zero Drama Bento — 6s
Three translucent glass cards slide in one by one. 
Card 1: "QR tickets, zero drama" with a QR code icon.
Card 2: "Pay with ease" with a credit card icon.
Card 3: "Sell in minutes" with a ticket icon.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cards arrive sequentially.
Audio intent: Rhythmic, satisfying ticks that match the music beat as each card lands.
Audio-coupled idea: Card-by-card sequence aligned to the beat.
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Event — 3s
A single, beautiful event card takes center stage. It features a dark cover image, a floating date badge ("DEC 12"), and elegant typography.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: A moment of appreciation, smooth and cinematic.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: smooth crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
The text "Selling out has never been this ridiculously easy." appears briefly. It fades out to reveal the simple, elegant "TixNaija" text logo centered, with a subtle ember streak sweeping across it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: Finality and elegance. The music fades out smoothly.
Audio-coupled idea: none

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic and polished
**Audio summary:** A sophisticated, driving beat that supports smooth UI reveals, with tactile, premium sound effects for interactions.
