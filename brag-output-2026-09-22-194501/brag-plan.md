# Brag Plan: TixNaija

## What is this app?
TixNaija is a seamless event ticketing platform for discovering, buying, and hosting events in Nigeria with QR code access.

## The angle
The feeling of zero friction when buying a ticket to the hottest event in Lagos. It's clean, modern, and instantly secure with cryptographic QR tickets.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big text: "Buy tickets." (Hold) "Turn up." (Ember glow sweeps across the text).

## Key moments (the middle)
- The search bar expanding with "Discover events..."
- A sleek ticket card sliding in showing "QR tickets, zero drama".
- "Pay with ease" sliding into view with supported payment methods.

## Outro / punchline
"Selling out has never been this easy." (TixNaija logo glow).

## User flow worth showing
Search for an event → Secure ticket (Pay) → Receive the scannable QR ticket.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clean, premium iOS-style app showcase
- Interpretation: smooth transitions, bright colors against dark background, snappy motion without chaos.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 30

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: oklch(0.14 0.02 40)
- Accent: oklch(0.85 0.15 85)
- Text: oklch(0.97 0.02 80)
- Display font: Cinzel, serif
- Body font: Inter, sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: The ember orange glow and glass UI cards.

## Share copy (draft)
Discover events, secure your spot, and turn up with TixNaija. QR tickets and seamless payments built for Nigeria.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: upbeat afrobeat or modern pop bed
- Music treatment: consistent volume, fade out at the end
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; background glow pulses slightly with the beat
- SFX posture: moderate; swooshes for slides, pop for the QR ticket
- Audio-coupled moments: 
  - QR ticket reveal — pop sound
  - Text reveal — subtle key ticks
- Restraint rule: keep it clean, no chaotic sounds

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Big display text "Buy tickets." appears. Then "Turn up." fades in below with the ember orange gradient. The background has a slow-spinning, subtle orange glow.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: clean entrance, establish the premium feel.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: upbeat afrobeat or modern pop bed starts.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Search — 4s
The search bar from the hero section slides up. The text "Find the hottest events in town" is typed into the search bar.
Sequential/interaction: typing simulation.
Audio intent: focus on the user action.
Audio-coupled idea: keyboard typing clicks.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Payment — 4s
A glass UI card with "Pay with ease" slides in, followed by logos/text for Cards, transfers, USSD.
Sequential/interaction: the payment methods appear one by one.
Audio intent: satisfying, secure feeling.
Audio-coupled idea: soft "tick" for each payment method.
Transition mood: smooth crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — QR Ticket — 4s
A digital ticket card with a large QR code scales in. Text reads "QR tickets, zero drama."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: the "payoff" moment, a satisfying pop.
Audio-coupled idea: single confident pop or ping.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
The TixNaija brand name appears in Cinzel font, glowing brightly. "Because turning up shouldn't be stressful." fades in below it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: resolved, professional ending.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: fades out smoothly.
