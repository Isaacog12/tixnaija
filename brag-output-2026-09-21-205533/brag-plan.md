# Brag Plan: TixNaija

## What is this app?
TixNaija is a premier ticket hub for discovering concerts and events in Nigeria, allowing organizers to sell tickets effortlessly with cryptographic QR codes for access control.

## The angle
The creative premise is a high-energy, polished Nigerian event launch. We're showcasing how ridiculously easy it is to find an event and get your unique QR ticket. The claim: "QR tickets, zero drama."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A bold, warm ivory screen with the Ember Orange glow. The words "Buy tickets." slam in, followed by "Turn up." in the gradient sunset text. An animated glowing orb sweeps across the background. 

## Key moments (the middle)
- The search bar UI animating "Find Events", simulating a user search.
- The "Coming up next" event cards sliding in one by one.
- The QR Code ticket value prop icon appearing with the text "QR tickets, zero drama."

## Outro / punchline
The "Turn up." sunset gradient phrase re-appears alongside the TixNaija logo or name, settling into the final shareable poster frame.

## User flow worth showing
1. Browse events: Simulate typing in the search bar.
2. Select event: The event cards cascade in.
3. Get ticket: The QR Code icon and "zero drama" claim pop in.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: slick, high-energy Nigerian event ticketing app
- Interpretation: Transitions will be smooth (slides and soft crossfades), motion will use Apple-grade spring easing, and text will have tight tracking and high legibility.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `oklch(0.98 0.008 80)` (light mode warm ivory)
- Accent: `oklch(0.66 0.22 40)` (Ember Orange)
- Text: `oklch(0.14 0.02 40)` (near-black warm)
- Display font: Cinzel
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: The sunset gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, oklch(0.75 0.18 50) 0%, oklch(0.66 0.22 40) 50%, oklch(0.5 0.24 15) 100%)`) and the floating glass cards.

## Share copy (draft)
Discover events across Nigeria and secure your spot in seconds. Buy tickets. Turn up. #TixNaija

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed / professional accents
- Music: upbeat Afrobeats or a warm corporate bed
- Music treatment: start at 0s, fade out at end
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS to make the background ember glow pulse.
- SFX posture: moderate; motion-matched to card arrivals and simulated clicks.
- Audio-coupled moments: typing in the search bar, cards sliding in one-by-one.
- Restraint rule: no chaotic or overly loud transitions; keep it elegant and polished.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 4s
The background is warm ivory with a subtle ember glow.
The text "Buy tickets." drops in using spring motion.
The gradient sunset text "Turn up." fades up below it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: establish energy
Audio-coupled idea: beat-aligned reveal of the two text lines
Music: upbeat warm bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Search — 4s
The search bar UI from the landing page appears centrally.
The text "Concerts in Lagos" is typed into the search bar.
A cursor clicks the "Find Events" button, which has the `glow-primary` effect.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate typing and clicking
Audio intent: active product use
Audio-coupled idea: typing sounds for each character, UI click sound
Transition mood: slide up → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Event Cards — 6s
Three event cards (mimicking the "Coming up next" section) stagger-slide in from the bottom.
They feature placeholder event images, dates, and titles.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards arrive one by one
Audio intent: showcase scale
Audio-coupled idea: card slide/whoosh sounds for each card arrival
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — QR Tickets — 4s
The screen clears. A stylized glowing QR code icon appears in the center.
The text "QR tickets, zero drama." lands below it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: satisfying conclusion
Audio-coupled idea: a solid UI lock or success chime as the QR code appears
Transition mood: none → End

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat
**Audio summary:** Starts energetic with the hook, uses UI sounds to emphasize the search and card reveals, and ends with a satisfying success note on the QR code.
